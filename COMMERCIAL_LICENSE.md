# Commercial & Government Licensing

**This canon is not free for commercial or government operational use.**

Public GitHub access is for reading, critique, and limited non-commercial sharing of **unmodified** copies under [LICENSE](LICENSE) **Section A**.

Anything beyond that requires **Section B** — a paid / written Commercial & Government License from the copyright holder.

---

## Why this structure exists

| Goal | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Public can read and argue with the living doctrine | Section A (free, narrow) |
| Stop free commercial appropriation / white-labeling | Section A non-commercial + no unauthorized derivatives |
| U.S. and other governments that want to operationalize it **pay** | Section B required for operational use |
| Still accept public contributions | Contributor assignment / exclusive license to copyright holder |

This is **source-available dual licensing**, not OSI “open source.”  
OSI open source cannot require the government to pay for ordinary use. That is intentional.

---

## Who needs Section B

You need a Commercial & Government License if you are (non-exhaustive):

- A **U.S. federal, state, local, tribal, or territorial** agency
- A **military, intelligence, homeland security, emergency management, or continuity** organization
- A **government contractor** using the canon in performance of a public contract
- A **company** selling training, software, consulting, standards packs, or products based on this doctrine
- Anyone creating **public derivatives**, official translations, or rebranded editions
- Anyone embedding substantial text into paid curricula or proprietary playbooks

**You do not need Section B** only to:

- Read the repo
- Cite it with attribution in non-commercial commentary or scholarship
- Share an **unmodified** link or copy for non-commercial discussion
- Open a critique issue or submit a PR under the contribution terms

## What Section B can include

Negotiated case-by-case:

| Term | Examples |
|---|---|
| Scope | Single agency, department-wide, multi-agency, commercial product line |
| Use rights | Internal operational use, training, derivative playbooks, public standards language |
| Modifications | Whether you may adapt text and under what attribution rules |
| Duration | 1 year, multi-year, perpetual (priced accordingly) |
| Fees | Flat license fee, annual subscription, royalty, pilot-then-scale |
| Support | Optional paid updates, briefings, tabletop design, legislative drafting support |
| Audit | Compliance reporting for large deployments |
| Exclusivity | Not default; only by explicit paid agreement |

**No implied license.** Publication on GitHub ≠ public domain ≠ free federal license.

## U.S. Government procurement (practical path)

1. **Identify the need** — continuity, preparedness, CISA/FEMA/NIST-aligned civil continuity training or doctrine support.  
2. **Contact the copyright holder** early (before writing an RFP that copies the text).  
3. **Negotiate Section B** — license fee and scope fit FAR/agency rules (commercial item license, sole-source only if lawfully justified, etc.).  
4. **Do not** paste large portions of this canon into official documents until licensed.  
5. **Ideas vs. expression** — copyright protects the wording/structure of these documents, not abstract ideas. Independent policy work is fine; copying or closely adapting this expression for operational programs is not free.

This file is **not legal advice** and does not create a contract. Section B terms control when signed.

## Pricing philosophy (indicative, not an offer)

Exact fees are negotiated. Directionally:

| Tier | Direction |
|---|---|
| Small nonprofit non-operational teaching | Often remains under Section A if unmodified + non-commercial |
| Commercial training / product | Paid Section B |
| Single local government pilot | Modest fixed fee + attribution |
| State / multi-site operational use | Annual license |
| Federal department / multi-agency operational use | Enterprise Section B — **this is the “pay to use it” tier** |
| Exclusive or work-for-hire adaptation | Premium; rarely granted |

**No token payment. No equity-for-license scams. Fiat or standard commercial invoicing.**

## Infringement

Unauthorized commercial use, government operational use, or derivative distribution outside Section A may be copyright infringement. The copyright holder reserves all enforcement rights, including takedowns, damages, and injunctive relief.

Good-faith readers and non-commercial critics are not the target. Free-riding vendors and unlicensed public programs are.
