# Civil Continuity Infrastructure (CCI)

### One-pager for Congressional staff — not a crypto bill

**Ask (near term):** Cosponsor or support **study + pilot** authority (or NDAA/homeland **report language**) directing CISA, FEMA, and NIST to map civil single points of failure and run 3–5 offline-first community pilots.  
**Do not ask (ever under this brand):** A government token, mandatory wallet, or investment product.

---

## Problem (non-hysterical)

Modern civil life concentrates payments, identity proofing, mobile comms, knowledge access, and increasingly AI-assisted decisions into a few failure domains. When the datacenter, clearing system, or tower fails, **official Continuity of Government does not restore a grocery line, a clinic formulary, or a neighborhood’s ability to coordinate.**

**Engineering principle:** A backup that fails for the same reason as the primary is not a backup (*independent failure domains*).

## Proposal

Treat **Civil Continuity Infrastructure** as complementary to COG/COOP:

| Capability | Plain English |
|---|---|
| SIGNAL | Comms that do not require one tower/ISP |
| LEDGER | Lawful value continuity / self-custody research—not a CBDC |
| NAME | Identity attestation when central registries are down |
| LIBRARY | Offline essential knowledge (water, med, power, ag) |
| MIND | Local open models for logistics/triage—no datacenter required |
| COUNCIL | Pre-agreed local recovery rules with sunsets & audits |

**COG protects the state’s ability to decide. CCI protects the people’s ability to endure.**

## What we want in statute (Phase 1)

1. Definitions + joint **SPOF study** (12–18 months).  
2. **Time-boxed pilots** (urban, rural, tribal, territorial).  
3. **Open-source / open-standards** conditions on federal R&D.  
4. **Non-endorsement clause:** no U.S. Government cryptocurrency; no mandatory digital asset holding.  
5. Anti-scam grant rules (no token marketing with public funds).

## What this is not

- Not a plan against any government  
- Not a token sale or “number go up” agenda  
- Not a surveillance identity grid  
- Not a replacement for banks, 911, or constitutional succession  

## Why now

Existing architecture already prioritizes critical infrastructure resilience and continuity modernization (e.g., CISA’s national coordinator role under NSM-22; FEMA continuity directives; ongoing national continuity policy reviews). CCI fills the **civil endurance** gap those instruments were not built to own alone.

## Proof over press releases

Prefer district **tabletop exercises** (e.g., multi-week grid + payments outage) and public after-action reports over influencer demos.
