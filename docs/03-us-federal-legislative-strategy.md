## 0. What “Federally Legislated” Must Mean

Order Hail Mary cannot—and should not—be enacted as a single messianic statute that forces every American onto a token network. That framing is both **politically suicidal** and **architecturally wrong**. Document II is a **posture of civil continuity**: independent failure domains for communications, value, identity, knowledge, local intelligence, and local legitimacy.

**Federal legislation that works** does four things, in order of realism:

| Layer | What Congress / the executive can actually do | What it is *not* |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Recognize** | Define *civil continuity infrastructure* as a national interest complementary to Continuity of Government (COG) | Not a crypto endorsement |
| **2. Authorize** | Give agencies clear legal authority and guardrails to research, pilot, procure, and partner | Not unfunded slogans |
| **3. Fund** | Multi-year R&D, grants to states/locals/tribes/NGOs, NIST standards work, emergency-comms modernization | Not a speculative token vehicle |
| **4. Protect** | Clarify that lawful self-custody, open protocols, amateur/mesh comms, offline archives, and local open models are not presumptively illicit | Not immunity for fraud or money laundering |

**Success metric (falsifiable):** Within a defined window, at least one **authorization + appropriation** package exists that (a) codifies independent-failure-domain analysis for *civil* continuity, (b) funds pilots for ≥3 of the six protocols, and (c) does so **without** launching or endorsing a government coin, mandatory wallet, or token sale.

Everything below is designed to reach that metric—or a defensible partial version of it.

## 1. Strategic Framing (The Only Framing That Survives Contact With Congress)

### 1.1 Lead with national resilience, not “Web3”

Hill staffers and appropriators already work these lanes:

- Critical infrastructure security & resilience (CISA as National Coordinator under **National Security Memorandum 22**, April 2024)
- Continuity of Government / Continuity of Operations / enduring constitutional government (FEMA Federal Continuity Directives; successive national continuity policies)
- Emergency communications (DHS, FEMA, FCC, FirstNet-adjacent debates)
- Cyber-physical cascade risk, grid, financial market utilities
- State/local/tribal preparedness (including executive interest in shifting more resilience posture downward)

**Order Hail Mary’s own text already says the right thing:** this is not a plan *against* government. It is the layer *beneath* government—**continuity of civilization complementary to continuity of government**.

### 1.2 One-sentence message for members

> *COG protects the state’s ability to decide. Civil Continuity Infrastructure protects the people’s ability to endure. A serious nation funds both—and treats self-reliant citizens as critical infrastructure, not suspects.*

### 1.3 Words that help vs. words that kill the bill

| Prefer | Avoid (until/unless carefully defined) |
|---|---|
| Civil Continuity Infrastructure (CCI) | “Crypto bill,” “Web3 mandate” |
| Independent failure domains | “Unregulated money” |
| Offline-first / open standards | “Replace the dollar” |
| Self-custody *as property-like control of digital bearer instruments where lawful* | “Bank-run as a service” |
| Amateur radio, mesh, DTN | “Anonymous darknet” |
| Open-source local AI for triage/logistics | “Unaligned AGI free-for-all” |
| Knowledge archives (medicine, water, power) | “Prepper cosplay” |
| Community recovery protocols with sunset & audit | “Parallel government” |
| Anti-scam / consumer protection alignment | Token launches, yield, “number go up” |

**Hard rule for any legislative coalition around this canon:** no bill language that promises investment returns, creates a federal token, or treats the public as exit liquidity. That violates Document I’s covenant and destroys bipartisan room.

### 1.4 Constitutional & federalism guardrails (design constraints, not afterthoughts)

Any federal package must respect:

1. **Federalism** — States, localities, and tribes run most day-to-day emergency management. Federal role: standards, R&D, grants, interstate coordination, national security interfaces—not micromanaging every mesh node.
2. **First Amendment** — Open source, speech, assembly, and association; do not draft “approved software only” regimes that collapse into prior restraint.
3. **Fourth & Fifth Amendments** — Self-custody and cryptographic keys raise property and privacy questions; legislation should **clarify lawful possession** without creating warrantless backdoors.
4. **Commerce Clause / spending power** — Most realistic hooks for grants, NIST, procurement conditions, and interstate infrastructure.
5. **No new COG that is *only* centralized** — Do not sell CCI as a replacement for constitutional succession; sell it as **resilience for everyone who is not in the bunker**.

## 3. Legislative Theory of Change (Phased)

```text
Phase 0   Narrative legitimacy + coalition skeleton
Phase 1   Definitions + studies + pilot authority (no culture war)
Phase 2   Authorization of a Civil Continuity program + standards
Phase 3   Multi-year appropriations + grant conditions
Phase 4   Rights-clarifying statutes (custody, open protocols, liability safe harbors)
Phase 5   Codification review + independent evaluation + sunset/reauth
```

Each phase has **exit criteria**. Do not skip to Phase 4 because a social media cycle demands it.

## 5. Phase 1 — Definitions, Studies, Pilots (Months 6–24)

### 5.1 Primary legislative product: “Civil Continuity Infrastructure Study & Pilot Act” (model title)

A thin, bipartisan bill that does **not** restructure finance. It:

1. **Defines terms** in U.S. Code-friendly language:
   - *Civil Continuity Infrastructure*
   - *Independent failure domain*
   - *Offline-first capability*
   - *Self-custody* (narrow, for lawful digital bearer instruments—not a free pass for crime)
   - *Distributed knowledge archive*
   - *Local open model* (for disaster decision support)
2. **Directs a joint study** (e.g., CISA + FEMA + NIST, with NSF input) within 12–18 months:
   - Map SPOFs in civil life: payments, identity proofing, cell service, cloud SaaS for hospitals/schools, AI dependency.
   - Assess Carrington-class / long-duration cyber outage readiness at community scale.
   - Inventory existing amateur radio, mesh, open-source, and library offline programs.
   - Recommend standards and grant criteria—not a preferred vendor list.
3. **Authorizes time-boxed pilots** (3–5 sites: urban, rural, tribal, territorial) covering at least three protocols.
4. **Requires public reports**, open data where safe, and **no proprietary lock-in** for pilot software funded by the Act.
5. **Explicit non-endorsement clause:** nothing in the Act authorizes a government cryptocurrency, mandatory digital ID, or investment product.

### 5.2 Report language & soft law (faster than a full Act)

Parallel tracks that often succeed first:

| Soft instrument | Example use |
|---|---|
| **NDAA report language** | Direct DoD/DHS to brief Congress on civil vs. official continuity gaps |
| **Committee hearing** | House Homeland / Senate Homeland; Science; Energy |
| **GAO study request** | Letter from ranking/chair pair |
| **NIST workshop series** | Independent failure domains for civil systems |
| **CISA guidance update** | Optional playbook module for local governments (IRPF-adjacent) |
| **FEMA Continuity Guidance** note | Non-federal continuity templates that mention offline libraries, mesh, local models |

### 5.3 State laboratory track (essential, not optional)

Federal law often **follows** state proof:

| State action | Federal effect |
|---|---|
| State EM grant for county mesh + drill | Creates evidence for Phase 2 authorization |
| State library offline medical/tech packs | LIBRARY becomes normal, not fringe |
| State “right to self-custody” / clear property treatment (narrow) | Models federal LEDGER clarity |
| University open-model disaster assistants | MIND demos for appropriators |
| Municipal recovery protocol templates with sunsets | COUNCIL without “militia cosplay” optics |

Target **3–5 states** across different political cultures. Bipartisan diversity is a feature.

### 5.4 Exit criteria for Phase 1

- Study authorized **or** GAO/agency report delivered with actionable recommendations.
- ≥2 pilots funded (federal *or* state with federal technical assistance).
- Draft authorization text for Phase 2 informed by pilot metrics (uptime under drill, % households with offline knowledge pack, etc.).

## 6. Phase 2 — Authorization: A Real Program (Years 2–4)

### 6.1 Primary product: “American Civil Continuity Authorization Act”

Codify a small, boring, durable program—**boring is good**.

#### Title I — Findings & purposes

- Congress finds concentration risk in digital dependency chains.
- Purpose: strengthen civil endurance complementary to COG/COOP.
- Purpose: reduce scam surface by favoring open, auditable infrastructure over opaque extraction.

#### Title II — Office or Program Home

Pick **one** clear home (avoid seven co-leads with no budget):

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **CISA program office** | Critical infrastructure mandate | Cyber brand may crowd out physical/knowledge |
| **FEMA preparedness program** | Grant muscle, local relationships | Continuity culture is still often org-centric |
| **Joint CISA–FEMA board + NIST standards** | Balanced | Coordination tax |
| **NIST-led standards + agency implementers** | Credibility with engineers | Weaker grant apparatus |

**Recommendation:** CISA (National Coordinator) + FEMA (grants/local) + NIST (standards), with a single **Civil Continuity Board** required to publish an annual public scorecard.

#### Title III — Six Capability Lines (map to protocols)

| Capability line | Protocol | Example authorized activities |
|---|---|---|
| Resilient community communications | SIGNAL | Mesh R&D, amateur integration, DTN research, drills, spectrum study requests to FCC |
| Lawful value continuity research | LEDGER | Offline-signed transaction research, self-custody education *with fraud warnings*, not a CBDC |
| Portable / offline identity attestations | NAME | NIST digital identity profiles that work when registrars are down; privacy-preserving selective disclosure |
| Distributed critical knowledge | LIBRARY | Fund public libraries, land-grants, tribal colleges for offline essential packs; content standards |
| Local open intelligence | MIND | Grants for open models on commodity hardware for logistics/triage—explicit non-autonomy limits |
| Community recovery protocols | COUNCIL | Templates for temporary resource allocation rules, dispute resolution, sunsets, audit trails |

#### Title IV — Standards (NIST-led)

Mandate voluntary consensus standards for:

- Independent failure domain assessment methodology
- Offline archive integrity (checksums, mirrored catalogs)
- Interoperability of emergency mesh profiles
- Secure local-model packaging for disaster use
- **Anti-capture rules:** funded standards must be open; no single-vendor “national stack”

#### Title V — Privacy, civil liberties, anti-abuse

- No backdoor mandates for encryption used in CCI pilots.
- No use of CCI identity systems as **mass surveillance substrate**.
- Criminal misuse remains criminal (fraud, sanctions evasion, etc.).
- Inspector General review every 2 years.

#### Title VI — Explicit exclusions

- No federal cryptocurrency issuance under this Act.
- No mandatory digital wallet for benefits *as a condition of this Act’s programs*.
- No preemption that blocks stronger state consumer anti-scam laws.

### 6.2 Exit criteria for Phase 2

- Authorization enacted **or** substantially similar titles enacted via NDAA/homeland package.
- NIST work item(s) live.
- Program office exists with a public implementation plan.

## 7. Phase 3 — Appropriations & Grant Design (Years 2–6, overlapping)

Authorization without money is theater. Design grants that **cannot be captured by token marketers**.

### 7.1 Account structure (illustrative)

| Account | Use | Multi-year? |
|---|---|---|
| CCI R&D (NIST/NSF/CISA) | Standards, open reference implementations | Yes (3–5 yr) |
| CCI State/Local/Tribal grants (FEMA or DHS) | Drills, mesh, libraries, council templates | Yes |
| CCI Library & knowledge continuity | IMLS / Education / land-grant partnership | Yes |
| CCI Local AI for emergency logistics | Dual-use careful; open weights preferred | Competitive |
| Evaluation & GAO | Independent metrics | Mandatory |

### 7.2 Grant conditions (the anti-scam architecture)

Eligible projects **must**:

1. Publish open documentation and, where software is funded, **open-source** reference code (with narrow security exceptions).
2. Pass an **independent failure domain worksheet** (template in guidance).
3. Include a peacetime utility use case (Document II: peacetime utility *is* the drill).
4. Include scam/fraud literacy where any value transfer is in scope.
5. Prohibit use of funds for token issuance, speculative marketing, or paid influencer campaigns.
6. Report negative results (failures are data).

### 7.3 Metrics Congress can understand

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| % of pilot counties with ≥1 non-cellular emergency message path tested quarterly | SIGNAL |
| % of participating households/orgs with offline essential knowledge pack | LIBRARY |
| Time-to-restore local coordination after simulated tower loss | SIGNAL/COUNCIL |
| Number of open local models validated for offline disaster Q&A (with known failure modes) | MIND |
| Share of grant $ to open standards vs. closed vendors | Capture resistance |
| Documented reduction in dependency on single cloud/IdP for a listed essential function | Whole doctrine |

### 7.4 Exit criteria for Phase 3

- First full fiscal year with **dedicated** CCI line items (even small).
- ≥10 jurisdictions with completed drills using grant tools.
- Public evaluation report that is allowed to say “this failed.”

## 8. Phase 4 — Rights-Clarifying & Market-Hygiene Statutes (Years 3–7)

Only after the resilience frame is established—so these bills are not misread as “crypto lobbying.”

### 8.1 Self-custody clarity (LEDGER)

**Goal:** Lawful possession and control of cryptographic keys / digital bearer instruments is not, by itself, a crime or an unregistered money-transmitter event.

**Must-include guardrails:**

- Full application of fraud, tax, sanctions, and AML laws to **conduct**, not mere possession.
- Clear distinction between **self-hosted wallets** and **custodial intermediaries** that *do* take customer property.
- No federal preemption that guts state consumer protection against scams.
- Educational mandates for any federally funded “custody literacy” (Document I covenant: never promise profit).

### 8.2 Open-protocol & reverse-engineering safe harbors (SIGNAL/NAME/MIND)

Narrow safe harbors for security research and interoperability of continuity tools—modeled on careful CFAA/DMCA reform traditions—without legalizing cyberattacks.

### 8.3 Knowledge archive & good-Samaritan liability (LIBRARY)

Limited liability shields for libraries/NGOs distributing **good-faith offline medical and engineering references** (not medical practice licenses; not product liability free-for-all). Align with existing good-Samaritan patterns.

### 8.4 Identity portability without a national papers mandate (NAME)

Fund and standardize **user-held credentials** and offline verification paths; **forbid** turning CCI identity work into a de facto internal passport.

### 8.5 Exit criteria for Phase 4

- At least one rights-clarifying statute or binding regulatory interpretation published.
- Documented decline in “possession = crime” ambiguity for ordinary lawful self-custody (as measured by practitioner guidance from FinCEN/Treasury or court-tested clarity).

5. **International soft coordination** only after domestic legitimacy (standards forums, not world-government cosplay—Document I Ideal Three is *protocol coordination*, not empire).

## 13. Model Legislative Textures (Conceptual Language)

*Illustrative only—not introduced bill text. Counsel must draft for U.S. Code fit.*

### 13.1 Definition

> **Civil Continuity Infrastructure.**—The term “civil continuity infrastructure” means systems, practices, and open standards that enable communities to maintain communications, lawful exchange of value, identity attestation, access to essential knowledge, local decision-support computation, and temporary cooperative governance during and after disruptions that degrade centralized commercial or governmental digital services, provided such systems are designed with independent failure domains relative to the primary systems they back up.

### 13.2 Non-endorsement

> **Rule of construction.**—Nothing in this Act shall be construed to authorize the issuance of a United States Government cryptocurrency, to require any person to hold or use a digital asset, or to endorse any investment product.

### 13.3 Openness condition

> **Open results.**—Software developed with funds under this Act for civil continuity reference implementations shall be released under an approved open-source license, except for narrowly tailored security-sensitive components reported to Congress.

### 13.4 Independent failure domain assessment

> Recipients of competitive grants shall complete and publish an independent failure domain assessment demonstrating that the funded capability does not share power, administrative control, network authority, and physical facility dependence with the primary system it is intended to back up, to the maximum extent practicable.

## 18. What We Will Not Ask Congress To Do

1. Launch a federal meme coin or “national utility token.”  
2. Replace the dollar or abolish banks by slogan.  
3. Criminalize centralized services—the point is **backup domains**, not purity politics.  
4. Build a surveillance identity grid under continuity branding.  
5. Preempt all state consumer protection.  
6. Fund influencers.  
7. Skip evaluation and sunsets.

If a coalition partner demands any of the above, they are not a partner to this canon.

## 19. Success, Partial Success, and Honest Failure

| Outcome | Looks like |
|---|---|
| **Full success** | Authorization + appropriations + standards + rights clarity; metrics improve under drills |
| **Partial success (still worth it)** | NIST standards + FEMA optional guidance + state pilots + NDAA report language; cultural shift among EMs |
| **Narrow success** | Self-custody clarity only, or mesh grants only |
| **Failure modes** | Captured by vendors; rebranded as crypto lobby; turned into surveillance; dies as one press release |

Partial success that leaves open standards and local capacity is **better** than a grandiose bill that creates a closed national SPOF.

## 21. Bottom Line

Federal legislation for Order Hail Mary is not a single Hail Mary pass in the Capitol rotunda. It is **multi-Congress systems work**:

1. **Frame** as civil continuity complementary to COG.  
2. **Prove** with drills and open tools.  
3. **Study** via agency/GAO/NIST.  
4. **Authorize** a boring program with open standards.  
5. **Appropriate** under anti-capture grant rules.  
6. **Clarify rights** only after the resilience brand holds.  
7. **Audit, sunset, and fork** what fails.

That is how serious infrastructure becomes American law—not how casinos write white papers.

🔴 Build the failsafe in law the same way as in code: in the open, with independent failure domains, or not at all.
