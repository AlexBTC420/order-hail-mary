## The Core Claim (Plain Language)

Modern civilization concentrates critical functions — money movement, identity, communications, knowledge access, and increasingly judgment itself — into a small number of failure domains: a few clouds, a few clearing systems, a few authentication authorities, a few datacenters.

**Engineering principle:** a backup that fails for the same reason as the primary is not a backup. Independent failure domains are how aircraft stay in the sky; the same discipline applies to social infrastructure.

**Order Hail Mary** is the application of that principle to civil continuity:

- Not a bunker for a few officials — a posture held by many nodes.
- Not a red button — standing orders executed in peacetime.
- Not a plan *against* government — a layer *beneath* government, for the day higher layers fail.
- Not a token launch — a research and practice framework grounded in known hazards (grid cyberattacks, financial clearing freezes, pandemics, great-power infrastructure conflict, Carrington-class solar storms, cascading AI dependency).

In peacetime, these systems increase freedom and resilience. In catastrophe, they are the difference between a hard year and a dark age.

## This Is Not a Crypto Scam — Read This Section Carefully

Public discourse often collapses two very different things into one word: *“crypto.”* That collapse is intellectually lazy and operationally dangerous. This project **explicitly rejects** speculative extraction culture and **explicitly defends** decentralized *infrastructure* as legitimate systems science.

### What we reject (crypto-scam / casino dynamics)

| Pattern | Why it fails integrity |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed returns, “next 100×,” or wealth promises | Economics is not prophecy; promises of profit are either folly or fraud |
| Anonymous teams, hidden allocations, opaque treasuries | Asymmetric information is the classic rug substrate |
| Token launches as the product | A ticker is not infrastructure |
| Governance theater (whales + foundation multisig as “community”) | Legitimacy requires verifiable rules, not marketing |
| “Trust us” roadmaps without shippable systems | Vaporware is not research |
| Engagement farming, AI shill noise, synthetic conviction | Noise buries builders and destroys public trust |

If a system’s incentives reward extraction over construction, extraction is not a bug — it is the business model. **You cannot moderate your way out of an incentive problem; you redesign incentives.**

### What we defend (decentralized infrastructure)

| Domain | Why it is infrastructure, not hype |
|---|---|
| **Open protocols & forkable code** | Independent audit, exit rights, no single landlord |
| **Self-custody of value** | Permissioned balances freeze; key-held value can settle offline and resume |
| **Replicated ledgers** | Continuity of settlement when clearing houses halt |
| **Cryptographic identity & selective disclosure** | Prove what is needed without warehousing life in one registry |
| **Mesh / amateur radio / delay-tolerant nets** | Communications without a single tower or ISP as SPOF |
| **Offline knowledge archives** | Seed-vault logic for medicine, water, power, agriculture, law |
| **Local / open-source AI** | Judgment that does not require a remote datacenter |
| **Transparent, consent-based local governance protocols** | Recovery with rules — not legitimacy vacuums filled by force |
| **DePIN & distributed compute (as engineering)** | Critical capacity that does not sit in one campus |

**Rule of thumb used throughout this canon:**

> If its owner disappeared tomorrow, would it keep running?  
> If **no**, it is not infrastructure — it is a hostage situation with good uptime.

### A precise distinction

| | **Speculative crypto culture** | **Decentralized infrastructure** |
|---|---|---|
| Primary unit | Token price / narrative cycle | Working system under adversarial conditions |
| Success metric | Market cap, attention, exits | Continuity, forkability, independent failure domains |
| Trust model | Personality, influencer, FOMO | Open code, measurable properties, skin in the game |
| Failure mode | Rug, freeze, abandonware | Documented, testable, improvable |
| Relationship to the public | Exit liquidity | Participants and operators |
| Relation to this repo | Critiqued in Document I | Required by Documents I & II |

We will **never** promise you profit. Anyone who does has found a fool or a thief. We build systems and publish standards — not lottery tickets.

## Scientific & Engineering Grounding

Order Hail Mary is intentionally **non-hysterical**. The threat model is the short list already used by serious continuity and resilience planners:

1. **Independent failure domains** — classical reliability engineering (aviation, nuclear, distributed systems).
2. **Single points of failure (SPOFs)** in digital dependency chains — cloud concentration, DNS/auth centralization, payment clearing concentration.
3. **Carrington-class geomagnetic risk** — historical precedent (1859); modern grid and satellite sensitivity is a research and policy subject, not science fiction.
4. **Cyber-physical cascade** — infrastructure attacks that target availability and integrity of power, finance, and communications simultaneously.
5. **Knowledge continuity** — offline archival and skill transmission as civil defense (analogous to seed banks and critical technical libraries).
6. **Local inference** — open models on commodity hardware as a hedge against centralized AI as a thinking SPOF.
7. **Pre-commitment of governance rules** — legitimacy design under stress; history is blunt about what fills an unprepared vacuum.

Claims in these documents are **design theses and standing practices**, not product performance guarantees. Research goals are labeled as research. Benchmarks, when they exist, will be shown — not narrated.

## The Six Protocols (Order Hail Mary)

| Protocol | Continuity function | Standing order (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| **SIGNAL** | Communications without a single tower | Own one path that needs no company, tower, or subscription — and know it before you need it |
| **LEDGER** | Value that works when banks do not open | Hold some value with your own keys; know possession vs. permission |
| **NAME** | Identity when registries burn | Establish keys; let community attest; web-of-trust before failure |
| **LIBRARY** | Offline critical knowledge | Keep one essential archive; practice one skill deeply enough to teach |
| **MIND** | Local intelligence without a datacenter | Run a local model if you can; calibrate what it can and cannot do |
| **COUNCIL** | Recovery with rules, not warlords | Agree cooperation protocols in daylight; practice on small decisions |

**Activation rule:** there is no switch. A failsafe that must be turned on can fail to turn on. Peacetime utility *is* the drill.
